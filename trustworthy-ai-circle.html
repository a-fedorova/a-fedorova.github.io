About the Circle

Join us to shape AI we can trust.

In a time when artificial intelligence is rapidly moving from experimentation into everyday practice, the gap between high-level principles and real-world implementation is becoming increasingly visible. While frameworks for trustworthy AI are evolving, their translation into concrete decisions, workflows, and products remains an open challenge.

Against this backdrop, the Trustworthy AI Circle creates a space for practitioners, researchers, and industry experts to collectively explore what responsible AI means in practice. The Trustworthy AI Circle is a recurring, in-person event series designed to foster exchange across disciplines and roles involved in the development and governance of AI systems. Each session focuses on a concrete use case — from LLM privacy to AI translation or agentic systems.
