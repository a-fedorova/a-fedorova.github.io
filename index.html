About

Bridging governance and engineering.

I work at the intersection of AI regulation, engineering practice, and safety-critical infrastructure. My research focuses on making trustworthy AI operational in real-world systems through clear requirements, risk governance, and practical implementation.

Alongside research, I organise the PhD Community of the School of Engineering and a monthly public meetup series on trustworthy AI, supported by the Digitalisation Initiative of the Canton of Zurich.

I am a researcher in responsible AI and AI governance at the Centre for Artificial Intelligence, ZHAW School of Engineering. My work explores how normative expectations for trustworthy AI can be translated into concrete requirements, engineering processes, and assessment instruments for safety-critical sectors such as rail, power grids, and air traffic management.

I contribute to the EU-funded AI4REALNET project and support the practical adoption of responsible AI through risk-based governance, requirements engineering, and cross-disciplinary collaboration between technical and organisational teams.

Building spaces for exchange.

I believe trustworthy AI is built in conversation, not in isolation. Since 2025 I have led the Trustworthy AI Circle, a monthly public meetup in Zürich where practitioners, researchers and industry experts work through concrete use cases together.

I have been part of Women in Data Science Zürich for three years, bringing established voices in data science and AI to share their knowledge with the Zürich community. At ZHAW, I organise the PhD Community of the School of Engineering, a role I took on two years ago to connect doctoral researchers across disciplines.

Beyond these recurring formats, I have co-organised single events with LatticeFlow AI and the ETH AI Center, and in 2026 I brought a workshop on governance in agentic AI to the Zürich AI Festival.
